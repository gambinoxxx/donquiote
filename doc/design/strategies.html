Live strategies send alerts. Test strategies run silently and record paper trades. Results are marked early until a strategy has 30 paper trades. Don't judge it before then.

If more than one strategy is live and they see the same setup (same pair and direction within 30 min), you get one alert tagged with both. Paper results use the same costs for every strategy. Past results don't guarantee future ones.
